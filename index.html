How We Met & Fell In Love

Our Story

❧
Chapter One · Balaji Temple

Where It All Began ❤️

It all began at Balaji Temple in Birmingham, where two paths crossed and the beginning of something truly special unfolded. From their very first meeting, there was an undeniable connection, a feeling that there was something different and special about one another. Little did they know that this simple meeting would mark the start of a beautiful journey filled with love, laughter and togetherness.

Chapter Two

Seven Days Was Too Long ❤️

It wasn’t long before they discovered that even a few days apart felt like a lifetime. After spending some time at his brother’s, he soon realised that he simply couldn’t stay away any longer. With a job interview as his reason to return and a heart that missed her far too much, he found his way back home. That was when they both realised that six or seven days apart was simply more than either of them could handle!

Chapter Three · Stratford-upon-Avon

Our Little Adventures, Our Big Forever ✨

After a busy week of barely getting to spend any time together, they decided to plan a little day out to Stratford-upon-Avon, a date they had both been looking forward to. Between the laughter, little disagreements and countless sweet moments, the day turned out to be even more special than they had imagined. It reminded them just how much they cherished each other’s company and how even the simplest adventures could feel magical when they were together. Somewhere along the way, they realised they didn’t just want more dates like this; they wanted a lifetime of them.

Chapter Five · Stafford

A Little Detour, A Lifetime Together 💍

Somewhere along the way, they jokingly decided that December 10th would be their wedding day, never imagining that one day they would actually be planning a forever together. Then came Stafford, where a missed train turned into an unexpected little adventure, a lovely stroll and a day of discovering even more about one another. What seemed like an ordinary detour became one of their sweetest memories. And now, they are turning those little dreams, shared jokes and unexpected coincidences into their very own happily ever after.

Their Forever

The Beginning of Their Forever 🤍

And here they are, ready to begin a beautiful new chapter together. From unexpected meetings and missed trains to little adventures, endless laughter and dreams shared, every moment has led them to this very special day.

Their story is far from over; in fact, this is where the best part begins. Here’s to more adventures, more memories, more little disagreements, countless laughs and a lifetime of finding home in one another.

Their next chapter awaits, and they cannot wait to write it together. ❤️

The Setting & Venue

The Reception in Birmingham

❧
★ Guest Reception & Banquet Venue
Celebratory Banquet & Party · 12:30 PM Till Midnight

Birmingham

West Midlands, B1, United Kingdom

All guests are cordially invited to celebrate with us in vibrant Birmingham! Join us for a lavish celebratory banquet, heartfelt speeches, live entertainment, cutting of the wedding cake, and dancing until midnight.

  • ✦ Guest Arrival: Welcome drinks & champagne reception from 12:30 PM.
  • ✦ The Banquet: Grand wedding feast & speeches at 1:00 PM.
  • ✦ Party & Dancing: Live DJ & evening dance floor until midnight carriages.
  • ✦ Transport & Stay: Minutes from Birmingham New Street Station & central hotels.
Directions to Birmingham

By Road: Birmingham is easily accessible via the M6, M5, and M42 motorways. Ample guest parking is available in central Birmingham car parks adjacent to the venue.

By Rail: Direct high-speed trains arrive into Birmingham New Street Station from London Euston (approx 1 hr 20 mins), Manchester Piccadilly (approx 1 hr 25 mins), and all major UK stations.

We suggest booking accommodation in Birmingham City Centre (ideal for the 12:30 PM reception, dining, and after the midnight carriages):

  • The Grand Hotel, Birmingham — Iconic luxury hotel on Colmore Row.
  • Radisson Blu / Hyatt Regency, Birmingham — Contemporary 4-star hotels close to the reception venue.
  • Malmaison Birmingham — Boutique hotel in the Mailbox district.

Evening festivities in Birmingham conclude at midnight. Taxis, Ubers, and private hire cars are readily available throughout Birmingham:

• Birmingham TOA Taxis: +44 (0) 121 427 8888
• Castle Cars: +44 (0) 121 472 2222
• Uber & Bolt: Available on demand across Birmingham
