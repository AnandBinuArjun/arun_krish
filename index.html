Our Story

❧
Autumn 2021 · London

Afternoon Tea in Bloomsbury

What was meant to be a swift cup of Earl Grey turned into five hours of ceaseless conversation, laughter over clotted cream scones, and the shared discovery of each other’s favorite books. By dusk, both knew something extraordinary had begun.

Summer 2023 · The Cotswolds

A Cotswold Weekend Getaway

Escaping city life for honey-stoned cottages, quiet country lanes, and evening walks along the willow-lined stream in Castle Combe. It was on this bridge that we spoke of the life and home we hoped to build together.

Spring 2025 · Gloucestershire

Under the Rose Bower

Surrounded by blossoming climbing roses in an ancient walled garden, Arun got down on one knee. With tears of pure joy, Krish said yes before the question was even fully finished.

The Setting

The Manor Gardens

Lower Slaughter Road, Bourton-on-the-Water
Gloucestershire, GL54 2EN, United Kingdom

Set amidst thirty acres of rolling private Cotswold parkland, ancient yew topiary, and fragrant heirloom rose gardens, the estate provides a peaceful, timeless English sanctuary for our celebration.

  • ✦ Ample complimentary guest parking on the private estate grounds.
  • ✦ Wheelchair accessible entrances, pathways, and restrooms.
  • ✦ Dedicated chauffeur greeting & cloakroom on arrival.
Get Directions

Direct trains run every hour from London Paddington to Moreton-in-Marsh Station (approx 1 hour 30 mins). From Moreton-in-Marsh, the venue is a pleasant 15-minute taxi ride through the rolling valleys. We recommend booking your station taxi ahead of time.

From London and the South East: Take the M40 to Junction 8, followed by the A40 toward Cheltenham. Follow signs to Bourton-on-the-Water. Enter via the North Gate where stewards will direct you to guest parking. Overnight parking is permitted until 11:00 AM on Sunday.

We have arranged preferential guest rates at nearby boutique hotels (quote "Arun & Krish Wedding" when booking):

  • The Slaughters Manor House (5 mins) — Luxury historic manor hotel.
  • The Rose & Crown Inn (8 mins) — Traditional Cotswold coaching inn.
  • Dial House Hotel, Bourton-on-the-Water (10 mins) — Charming garden rooms.

Because rural Cotswold taxis book out rapidly, please pre-book your midnight carriage at least one week in advance:

Dress Code & Details

❦

Gentlemen

Morning Dress, Black Tie or Dark Lounge Suit

Morning coats, sharp dark tuxedos, or classic tailored three-piece suits with neckties or bowties. Polished leather shoes.

Ladies

Formal Country Garden Attire or Evening Gown

Floor-length gowns or elegant midi dresses. Delicate florals, refined silhouettes, and fascinators/millinery are warmly welcomed. Block heels or wedges are recommended for the garden lawns.
